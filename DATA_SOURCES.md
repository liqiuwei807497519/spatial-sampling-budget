# Public release contents

The software is distributed under the MIT license. The public version includes source code, synthetic examples and metadata from public repositories. Native microscopy, clinical images, private expression matrices, study rat spot measurements and the internal author-review bundle are excluded.

* `synthetic_pilot.csv`, `example_sites.csv`, `example_blocks.csv` and `example_calibration.csv` contain constructed measurements. The notebook runs on synthetic data.
* `OMIX017533_files.csv` records the public NGDC file URLs, sample assignments and calculated SHA-256 values. The human expression matrices remain at the [originating repository](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/omix/release/OMIX017533).
* `reference_21479_to_20512.csv` contains gene symbols, stable gene identifiers and a feature-presence flag derived from the gene registries of public GSE230809, GSE160756 and GSE199866 releases. It contains no sample-level observations, coordinates, counts or clinical metadata. [GSE230809](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE230809) · [GSE160756](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE160756) · [GSE199866](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE199866).
* Third-party data remain subject to the applicable source terms. The software does not grant additional rights in those data.
