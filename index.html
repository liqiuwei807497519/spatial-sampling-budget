Assumptions and formulas

Each complete request has a marginal dual-detection probability p. Under independence, the chance of at least one success in m requests is 1 − (1 − p)m. With positive correlation, a shared beta propensity gives a failure probability of B(α, β + m)/B(α, β), where α = p(1/ρ − 1) and β = (1 − p)(1/ρ − 1).

The calculator finds the smallest integer meeting each target and labels values beyond 10¹² separately from a zero per-request success rate. Set the endpoint and denominator consistently in your pilot. Request-level dual detection can pool transcripts from different sites or barcodes. Use same-barcode co-detection probabilities for a same-barcode endpoint.
